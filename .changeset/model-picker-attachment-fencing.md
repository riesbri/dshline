Prevent the model, reasoning, and guided-setup commands from opening stale pickers, or changing the window's selected/default model, after their initiating attachment begins retirement. Their nested surfaces inherit that lifetime rather than outliving it: the ctrl-k subagent-authorization editor and the Connect browser are withdrawn with the gesture that opened them, without rolling back a settings write or a sign-in the reader had already submitted. Already-applied selections are preserved while late acknowledgements are suppressed, and a settled picker no longer leaves an abort listener on the attachment's signal.
