Prevent the model, reasoning, and guided-setup commands from opening stale pickers, or changing the window's selected/default model, after their initiating attachment begins retirement. Their nested surfaces inherit that lifetime rather than outliving it: the ctrl-k subagent-authorization editor, the Connect browser, and Connect's own action pickers, secret prompts, sign-in editor and route wizard are all withdrawn with the gesture that opened them, and admit no further settings or credential write. A write or sign-in the reader had already submitted is left alone rather than rolled back. Already-applied selections are preserved while late acknowledgements are suppressed, and a settled picker no longer leaves an abort listener on the attachment's signal.
